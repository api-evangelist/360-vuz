# 360 VUZ

> VUZ (360 VUZ) is an immersive social video platform delivering live and on-demand 360-degree and VR content across sports, concerts, entertainment, celebrities, travel, and master classes, accessible on mobile, tablet, desktop, and VR headsets including Apple Vision Pro. VUZ is headquartered in Riyadh (KAFD) with offices in Dubai, Amman, and Los Angeles, and is backed by 500 Global. Content creators and partners integrate via the 360VUZ Creator API.
